GOOD TO KNOW

Before You Book

What areas do you cover?

POSE serves Rizal and Metro Manila. Transport fees may apply depending on the venue.

Can the layout match our theme?

Yes. Layouts can be customized to your colors, motif, names, logos, or company branding.

Do you provide setup and attendants?

Yes. Setup, dismantling, and on-site attendants are included according to the selected package.

How many trading cards are included?

The ₱9,999, 3-hour Trading Card Photobooth package includes up to 75 finished, laminated cards with protective cases. The package includes up to four designs, with a fixed team assigned to each template. Additional cards or sessions require a separate quotation.

Can guests personalize their trading cards?

Yes. Guests can enter their names and select a special ability. Each card also has a unique card number. Team selection follows the chosen template design.

Can I add Event Photography to my photobooth booking?

Yes. Event Photography is a ₱2,500 add-on to eligible POSE main packages, with up to 3 hours of coverage, one photographer, standard editing and digital delivery. It is not offered as a standalone package; availability and staffing are confirmed when you inquire.

What is included in the projector add-on?

The +₱2,000 add-on includes a projector, screen, laptop and operator for up to 4 hours at the same event. Client-provided presentations, videos and slideshows can be played; sound system and content production are not included.

Can the projector be used in a bright or outdoor venue?

Projectors work best in indoor spaces with controlled lighting. We confirm screen size, placement, power and venue brightness before accepting a projector booking.

Does clicking “Check Availability” reserve the date?

No. Submitting an inquiry is not a reservation. Our team will confirm availability, requirements and quotation before a booking is accepted.

How do I book the AI Photobooth?

Choose AI Essential (₱7,499 / 2 hours / up to 75 portraits) or AI Premium (₱9,999 / 3 hours / up to 150 portraits). Each completed AI session includes one transformation and one 4R print. The AI allocations are fixed per booking, not unlimited sessions; repeat generations consume additional credits. Reliable venue internet is required for AI processing and sharing.

Can we extend the service time?

Yes, subject to availability. Extra-hour rates depend on the package.
