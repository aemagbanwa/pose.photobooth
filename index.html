GOOD TO KNOW

Before you book.

What areas do you cover?

POSE serves Rizal and Metro Manila. Transport fees may apply depending on the venue.

Can the layout match our theme?

Yes. Layouts can be customized to your event colors, motif, names, logos, or company branding.

Do you provide setup and attendants?

Yes. Setup, dismantling, and on-site attendants are included according to the selected package.

Can we extend the service time?

Yes, subject to availability. Extra-hour rates depend on the package.
