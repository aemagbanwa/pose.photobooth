GOOD TO KNOW

Before You Book

What areas do you cover?

POSE serves Rizal and Metro Manila. Transport fees may apply depending on the venue.

Can the layout match our theme?

Yes. Layouts can be customized to your colors, motif, names, logos, or company branding.

Do you provide setup and attendants?

Yes. Setup, dismantling, and on-site attendants are included according to the selected package.

How do I book the AI Photobooth?

Choose AI Photobooth in the inquiry form and send us your event details. We’ll share packages, pricing, and availability, and help you choose three AI portrait styles. A stable, fast internet connection is required at the venue for AI processing and sharing.

Can we extend the service time?

Yes, subject to availability. Extra-hour rates depend on the package.
