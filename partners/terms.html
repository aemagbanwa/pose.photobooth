Affiliate program terms & privacy notice

Draft program rules — review and approve before public launch. Registration is free and subject to manual approval. At registration, affiliates provide a GCash/Maya account name, wallet number and payment QR image for commission payout. QR images are stored privately and shown only to the account holder and authorized POSE administrators. Affiliates must supply accurate wallet details and verify changes. Only unique, attributable referrals recorded with a valid code qualify. Existing client claims, self-referrals and duplicate commissions are not permitted. POSE makes final decisions after reviewing booking records.

A booking becomes commission-eligible only after the event is completed and fully paid. Cancelled, refunded and unpaid bookings do not qualify. Base commissions depend on the agreed package; transportation, add-ons and extensions do not automatically increase commission. Custom quotes require approval.

Monthly tier thresholds are Starter (1–2 completed and paid bookings), Silver (3–5, +10%), Gold (6–10, +20%) and Platinum (11+, +30%). The bonus applies to base commissions, not booking value, and recalculates retroactively for eligible bookings within the same calendar month. New months start at Starter. Payouts require reconciliation, and a ₱500 minimum payout may apply; unpaid earnings carry forward. Payout dates target the 15th and 30th, subject to verification and applicable taxes.

Affiliates must not misrepresent prices, promise availability, spam customers or use POSE branding in unapproved advertising. POSE may suspend or terminate affiliates for abuse. Disputes are reviewed against POSE booking and payment records.

Privacy notice

POSE collects affiliate identity, contact information, partner category, optional social profile, account status, referral and payout records to administer the program, verify applications, prevent abuse and process commissions. Information is accessed by authorized POSE administrators and necessary service providers (for example, Google services used for email and records). We do not publicly disclose affiliate payout or customer contact information. Contact pose.photobooth.2017@gmail.com for privacy questions, corrections or data requests. Retention and statutory obligations should be reviewed before launch.
